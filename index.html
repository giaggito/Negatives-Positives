Negatives & Positives

Two free Mac apps for film photographers. Turn your negatives into photos, and edit every picture with a modern, intuitive editor inspired by analogue photography.

Negatives

Photograph or scan your negatives, open them, done.

  • Open a whole roll at once

  • Works with camera RAW files and scanner files

How to install

Download the app with the button above. It goes to your Downloads folder.

Open the downloaded file. A small window appears.

Drag the app onto the Applications folder in that window.

Open Applications in the Finder and double-click the app.

The first time you open it

The apps are free and made by one person, so they are not registered with Apple. Your Mac asks you to confirm once:

  1. Double-click the app. A message says Apple could not check it. Click Done.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down.
  3. Click Open Anyway next to the app's name, and enter your Mac password.
  4. Click Open. From now on it opens like any other app.

On macOS 14 Sonoma you can also right-click the app → Open → Open.
