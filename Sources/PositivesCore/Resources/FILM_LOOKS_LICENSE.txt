FilmLooks.lzfse — colour tables of the film looks in Positives
===============================================================

This file is an adaptation (converted, smoothed of 8-bit quantisation, resampled to 48³ and re-encoded) of
look-up tables from two collections. Film and brand names are trademarks of their owners; they are used only
to say which film a look approximates. Neither the authors of these collections nor this project are
affiliated with or endorsed by the companies that own the trademarks.

   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
